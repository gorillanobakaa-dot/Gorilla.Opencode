# v0.1.110 - multi-select, tombstoned hiding, /purge and /update, and the link sampler that was never called

> Session record generated 2026-08-20

---

## Problem Being Solved

The model picker had one cursor (selectedIdx) and no marked set, so assembling a shortlist from a 300-entry provider was 300 separate decisions. There was no removal at all, no in-session refresh, and no way to reset. Separately, the passive link-speed sampler shipped in v0.1.108 and v0.1.109 was dead code: beginLinkSample was defined and called by nothing.

## Approach Taken

Hiding is a PERSISTED TOMBSTONE, not a deletion, because deletion cannot work here: SupportedModels is seeded from eleven compiled-in provider maps and merged into by every refresh with maps.Copy, and the only deletions in the codebase are two local-endpoint teardown paths. RefreshOpenRouter even computes a Removed list and discards it. A runtime delete is undone by the next launch and the next refresh.

Filtering happens at BROWSE time (getModelsForProvider), not in the registry, so a session already running on a hidden model keeps working and hidden ids still resolve to names in the transcript.

The sampler was repaired by restoring a bracketing RoundTripper - the socket counter supplies bytes, but something must mark when a response starts and ends.

## Before

modelDialogCmp had selectedIdx and no marked field. getModelsForProvider returned every model for a provider unfiltered. visibleRows() capped at 30. No hidden-models store. No /purge or /update. linksample.go had beginLinkSample and countingDialContext, with no caller for the former.

## After

marked map[models.ModelID]bool on the dialog; x/a/d/H bound. hidden-models.json with Hide/Unhide/UnhideAll. getModelsForProvider skips config.IsModelHidden. ProviderHidden virtual column, sorted second after bookmarks. PurgeFetchedCatalogues drops only OpenRouter and Antigravity entries and their cache files. /purge and /update registered AND dispatched. visibleRows() has no ceiling. linkSampleTransport restored and wired outermost.

## Known Alternatives

Deleting from SupportedModels: rejected, see above - it silently undoes itself. Filtering in the registry instead of at browse time: rejected, it would break a session already running on a hidden model. Auto-hiding models that error: rejected by the owner and by evidence - five models in the same day's benchmark returned unreachable and could not be distinguished from our own exhausted quota.

## Decisions Made

- 📄 **Hiding is a tombstone honoured at browse time** — Deletion is undone by the next launch and refresh; filtering in the registry would break a live session
- 📄 **Purge spares compiled-in providers, bookmarks and the hidden list** — Owner: OpenRouter, Antigravity and ChatGPT models all work when signed in with Gmail; purge is about volume, not rot
- 📄 **Nothing auto-hides** — Five models returned unreachable the same day and could not be distinguished from an exhausted quota
- 🤖 **Marks keyed by ModelID, not index** — They must survive a provider change, a search, and the list rebuilding underneath
- 📄 **No change to the coder prompt** — Section ablation changed nothing (4/4) while other models on the identical prompt behaved correctly

## Tried and Abandoned

- **Adding /model to the busy-command allowlist** — TestSendingCommandsAreStillGated refused it and was right: choosing a model calls CoderAgent.Update and swaps the provider under a live request
- **A footer suffix of 23 bytes** — Pushed the worst-case label to 42 bytes against a documented 32-byte cap; it would have wrapped into a second row
- **A middot separator in that suffix** — U+00B7 is East Asian Ambiguous and banned by styles/ascii.go
- **!scripts/foo.py under an ignored /scripts/** — Git cannot re-include a file whose parent directory is excluded; the rule had to become /scripts/*
